# Privacy Policy for AIME TECH MEDIA LIMITED

## Introduction

Welcome to AIME TECH MEDIA LIMITED, a leading provider of AI-powered media generation services. At AIME TECH MEDIA, we are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This privacy policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your data when you use our AI media generation services.

## Information Collection and Use

We collect the following types of data to provide you with our AI media generation services:

1. **Account Information**
   - **What We Collect**: Your name, email address, and any other information you provide when creating an account.
   - **Purpose**: To manage your account, provide customer support, and deliver our AI media generation services.
2. **Media Generation Data**
   - **What We Collect**: Information about the images, audio, and video content you generate using our AI services, including prompts, preferences, and generated media files.
   - **Purpose**: To improve our AI models, provide better generation results, and enhance your user experience.
3. **Usage Analytics**
   - **What We Collect**: Data about how you interact with our AI generation tools, including feature usage, generation patterns, and service preferences.
   - **Purpose**: To optimize our AI algorithms and improve service performance.
4. **Device Information**
   - **What We Collect**: Technical information about your devices, including hardware specifications, operating system, and browser type.
   - **Purpose**: To ensure optimal performance of our AI generation services across different platforms.
5. **Payment Information**
   - **What We Collect**: Your payment method details, billing address, and transaction history.
   - **Purpose**: To process payments for our AI media generation services and maintain your subscription.

## Data Storage and Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information and generated media content. All data is encrypted during transmission and storage. We use secure cloud infrastructure and regular security audits to maintain the integrity of our systems. However, please note that no method of data transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

## AI Model Training and Data Usage

- We may use anonymized and aggregated data to improve our AI models
- Your generated content may be used to enhance our AI algorithms, but only after removing any personal identifiers
- You retain rights to the media content you generate using our services

## Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your data only in the following circumstances:

- With service providers who assist in operating our AI generation platform
- To comply with legal requirements
- To protect our rights and prevent misuse of our services
- With your explicit consent

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy periodically to reflect changes in our services or legal requirements. We will notify you of any significant changes through our website or email notifications.
